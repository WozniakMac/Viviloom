# OpenSims

An original life-simulation sandbox built with Godot 4.7, modelled on how classic life
sims look, feel and play. All art, sound and music are generated by the scripts in
`tools/` (Blender for models, pure Python for audio); nothing is copied from another game.

## Running

- Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5, or run `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .`
- Tests: `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --path . res://tests/run_tests.tscn`
- Checks (print OK/FAIL): `... --headless --path . -- --scenario=<saveload|travelcheck|householdscheck|audiocheck>`;
  `inputcheck` clicks through the real UI, so run it without `--headless`
- Screenshot of a scripted moment: `... --path . -- --screenshot=C:/path/out.png --scenario=<name>`
  (see `_run_scenario` in `scripts/main.gd` for the list)
- Start straight in Create a Sim: `... -- --scenario=new`; skip the title screen: `--scenario=skip_cas`
- Regenerate assets: `blender --background --python tools/make_sim.py`,
  `blender --background --python tools/make_objects.py`, `python tools/make_audio.py`

## What's in it

- **Title screen** with Play, Manage Households (several families per world), New Game.
  Households you are not playing live on: they age, work, get promoted, have babies
  and pass away, with neighbourhood news.
- **Create a Sim**: households of up to four, name, age (child to elder), body sliders,
  hairstyles, walk styles, skin/hair/outfit colours for Everyday, Sleepwear, Athletic and Swimwear,
  fourteen traits with conflicts, and an aspiration.
- **Neighbourhood map**: buy a furnished home or an empty plot; homes of other families
  are marked as taken.
- **Live mode**: six needs, emotions from moodlets with body language, 16 skills (fishing, piano, comedy, mischief, mixology, programming and more), wants
  and aspiration milestones with a Rewards Store, socials in five categories (Friendly,
  Funny, Romance, Mischief, Mean) and relationships (friends, going steady, break-ups,
  WooHoo, marriage, moving in), 8 careers with daily tasks and on-the-job choices, plus school, homework, life stages, babies,
  old age, death and the Grim Reaper, bills and power cuts, messes, breakdowns and fires,
  cooking recipes and group meals, held props, inventory with paintings and fish, phone (invite, pizza, travel),
  townies, visitors at the door, day and night, music and ambience.
- **Build/Buy**: walls and a one-drag room tool, roofs (gable, hip, flat or none, six colours), doors, windows, picket fences and gates, paint, floors, pools, a second storey with stairs,
  ~40 objects with colour swatches, and a scrolling catalogue.
- **Lot traits**: up to three per home (Homey, Chef's Kitchen, Gremlins, Natural Light...).
- **Community lots**: a park, gym, library and lounge.

## Controls

Left-click to walk or open an object's pie menu (some have sub-menus), click a Sim to
socialize, click a queued action to cancel it, P / 1-3 for speed, F3 build, F1 live,
V walls, Page Up/Down floors, Esc menu, WASD/arrows to pan, Q/E or right/middle-drag to
rotate, wheel or Z/X to zoom.
